## What the video covers

1. **The problem.** SAIL fixing single spot voyages daily, and what that costs.
2. **The solution.** Entering one real cargo lot (tonnes, origin, destination, laycan, term)
   into the Voyage Desk.
3. **The verdict.** The LOCK/WAIT recommendation, the forecast fan, and the "why this verdict"
   explanation, including what the option to keep waiting is worth in $/day.
4. **Physical reality.** The fleet-mix frontier rejecting classes with the binding reason, and
   port constraint checks at both ends.
5. **Risk.** The live risk feed, chokepoints on the traced route, and the carbon-intensity
   projection.
6. **The moats.** Port Twin, the Tonnage Field, the Fragility flip-point sweep, and the
   append-only decision ledger.
7. **Live deployment.** The same build running at https://sih-2026-nu-lake.vercel.app
